# Example library credits

The pictures in `examples/library/` were made by other people. They are not covered by this
repository's own licence; each keeps the licence listed below. Every file is either in the public
domain or under a Creative Commons licence that allows sharing and changes:

- **Public domain / CC0**: no conditions.
- **CC BY** (2.0 to 4.0): credit the author, link the licence and say what was changed.
- **CC BY-SA** (2.0 to 4.0): the same, and anything adapted from these files must be shared under
  the same licence. The CC BY-SA files stay CC BY-SA here. Resizing them doesn't change that.

All files were downloaded from the sources listed, then resized where needed and saved as JPEG for
this repository (one album is a PDF made of such JPEG pages): long side at most 1600 px for photos and artworks, 1280 px for the portraits in `People/`
and 1400 px for comic pages. GPS data was removed. The author and licence are also written into each
image's EXIF *Artist* and *Copyright* fields.

**People in the photos.** `People/` and one picture in `Inbox/` show public figures (NASA astronauts) in
official public-domain photos by US government agencies. These photos are free of copyright, but the
people in them still have personality rights. Don't use them in a way that suggests they endorse anything.

**Pepper&Carrot.** The Pepper&Carrot comics and artworks are by David Revoy, under CC BY 4.0
(<https://www.peppercarrot.com/en/about/index.html#license>). He also asks that his work not be used for AI
derivations
(<https://www.davidrevoy.com/article1178/license-update-ai-derivation-prohibited-on-all-my-art-lore-stories-and-comics>).
Respect that for his pictures here, including his Wikipe-tan drawing. Don't put them through AI-based
edits in screenshots, demos or tests: no generated or redrawn content, no automatic retouching or
recolouring, no AI cut-outs. Tagging, search, face and character recognition and duplicate finding
only analyse the pictures, so those are fine.

**Wikipe-tan** is the Wikipedia mascot character designed by Kasuga. The drawings of her by
different artists are all CC BY-SA.

Nothing here implies that any author or person shown endorses this project.
